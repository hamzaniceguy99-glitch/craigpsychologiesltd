Our services, in detail

Four services for children, families and schools, with informed consent before any assessment.

Program 01 · Why it's hard, not just that it is

Psychological assessment

Assessment combines standardised measures with observation, school information and what parents already know. The purpose is to explain why a child is finding something difficult and what would help, not to produce a score. Where another professional is needed — a paediatrician, a speech and language therapist — we say so.

  • Children and young people
  • Fee agreed in writing
  • Written consent

Program 02 · Advice staff can use

Consultation for schools

Much of what helps a child happens in a classroom without a psychologist present. Consultation gives school staff a structured way to think about a pupil and leave with specific, practical strategies — and sometimes concludes that assessment isn't needed at all.

  • Per session
  • Schools and settings
  • Quoted in writing
  • Staff time

Program 03 · Plain language

Work with parents and carers

Families often hold several reports and no clear picture. We go through what the findings actually mean, what is likely to help at home and at school, and what questions to put to the school or local authority — without promising outcomes nobody controls.

  • Parents and carers

Program 04 · Independent, and honest

Advice for statutory processes

Independent advice for statutory processes, written to the standard expected and clear about what the evidence supports. We are independent of any local authority or school, and we won't write a conclusion the assessment doesn't justify — including when a family hopes for a particular outcome.
